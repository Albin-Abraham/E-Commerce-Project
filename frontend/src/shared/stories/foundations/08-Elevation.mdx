# 🌓 Elevation

Elevation communicates spatial depth, surface hierarchy, and floating states along the z-axis.

---

## 01 — Principles

### 1. Light Source Consistency
All drop shadows cast downwards with a natural blur spread, simulating a single directional top ambient light source.

### 2. Dark Mode Surface Elevation
In dark themes, elevation is communicated through **surface lightening** rather than drop shadows:
- Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas): `#0b0f17`
- Surface Level 1 (Card): `#111827`
- Surface Level 2 (Popover): `#1f2937`
- Surface Level 3 (Modal): `#374151`

---

## 02 — Specification

| Elevation Level | Token | Offset & Blur | Primary Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 0** | `elevation.level0` | `none` | Base background |
| **Level 1** | `elevation.level1` | `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` | Cards, input containers |
| **Level 2** | `elevation.level2` | `0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` | Dropdowns, menus |
| **Level 3** | `elevation.level3` | `0 10px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` | Sticky navigation, side drawers |
| **Level 4** | `elevation.level4` | `0 20px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` | Modal dialogs |
| **Level 5** | `elevation.level5` | `0 20px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` | Top-level toasts, notifications |

---

## 03 — Tokens

Defined in `tokens/primitives/elevation.json` and `tokens/primitives/effects.json`.

---

## 04 — Implementation

```tsx
<div className="bg-surface shadow-[var(--shadow-md)] rounded-lg p-6">
  <Heading level={3}>Elevated Card</Heading>
</div>
```
